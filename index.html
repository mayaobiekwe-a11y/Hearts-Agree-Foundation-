One diaspora. One ecosystem. One Nigeria.

There is no shortage of diaspora Nigerians who care deeply about home. What has been missing is a place to build together. When hearts agree, what we build together outlasts what any of us could build alone.

What we do.

  1. 01

    Community Care

    Through We Remember You, our flagship program, we bring groceries, medical care, and direct support to women and families in shelters across Nigeria, and it grows every year.

  2. 02

    Economic Empowerment

    Through the Hearts Agree Entrepreneur Fund, we put grants, connections, and support behind diaspora members and Nigerian community builders who want to lead something lasting back home.

  3. 03

    Collective Action

    Through the Hearts Agree Task Force, we convene diaspora professionals, entrepreneurs, and investors committed to coordinated, long-term investment in Nigeria's future.

  4. 04

    Infrastructure & Development

    Through Infrastructure & Development Partnerships, we connect diaspora engineers, architects, and public health experts with Nigerian projects that need their skills.
