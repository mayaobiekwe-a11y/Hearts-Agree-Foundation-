Mission

We believe that crossing over does not mean leaving behind. What we build together, across distance, across borders, across generations, is stronger than what either side could build alone.

What We Do

Three ways the diaspora moves together.

Community Care

Through We Remember You, we show up for survivors and vulnerable women in Nigeria, not as outsiders, but as family who remembers.

Economic Empowerment

Through the Hearts Agree Entrepreneur Fund, we invest in diaspora members and community builders who want to run programs back home.

Collective Action

Through the Hearts Agree Task Force, we convene diaspora leaders who are done watching from the sidelines.

Founding Statement

“How can I have if my people don't have?”

Daniel Obiekwe

That question is the foundation of everything we do. Hearts Agree Foundation was born from a promise made in Lagos, and from the belief that memory is not passive. It asks something of us.
