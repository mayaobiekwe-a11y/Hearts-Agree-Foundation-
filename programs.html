Strategic Initiatives

Our Strategic Initiatives & Programs

Hearts Agree Foundation works across four interconnected strategic initiatives and programs. Each is rooted in the same belief: that what we do because we remember is the most powerful thing we can offer.

We Remember You is our flagship community care program, and it's not going anywhere. But the Hearts Agree Task Force and the Entrepreneur Fund are just as core to what we're building: the ecosystem that turns one-off generosity across the diaspora into coordinated, lasting development. The Ecosystem

Three ways the diaspora builds beyond We Remember You.

  • Economic Empowerment

    Hearts Agree Entrepreneur Fund

    Invests in diaspora members and Nigerian community leaders who want to build care programs and community initiatives back home. Grants, connections, and support for builders who are already moving. Not charity.

    Applications open in 2027.
    Apply for a grant
  • Diaspora Activation

    Hearts Agree Task Force

    A diaspora brain trust: Nigerian professionals, entrepreneurs, investors, and community leaders abroad, committed to coordinated, sustained investment in Nigeria's future. For diaspora Nigerians ready to do more than donate.

    Join the Task Force
  • Systems & Built Environment

    Infrastructure & Development Partnerships

    Connects diaspora professionals (engineers, architects, planners, public health experts) with Nigerian development projects and institutions that need their skills. The diaspora is one of Nigeria's greatest untapped resources.

    Partner with us
