Our Story

From one promise to an ecosystem.

We Remember You started as one visit to one shelter. Three years in, it's still our flagship program — but it also taught us something bigger than itself, and that's how Hearts Agree Foundation came to be.

The Promise

A shelter in Lagos, 2023.

Our founder stood in a shelter in Lagos, Nigeria, surrounded by women who had survived things most people will never have to imagine. She had come to give. What she left with was a promise: to come back, to bring more, and to make sure these women — and others like them — would know that someone on the other side of the world had not forgotten them.

That promise became We Remember You: an annual community care event for survivors of domestic violence and vulnerable women and families in Nigeria.

To feel remembered during your darkest hour is a deep act of humanity — and the true meaning of community.

Three Years In

What year one, two, and three looked like.

Year One · Dec 2023

The Beginning

We Remember You began with a single visit to a shelter in Nigeria. Our founder brought gifts and cash donations for 25 women, and a talk about remembering and being remembered. At the end, she made a promise: "I'll be back. And I'll bring more."

Year Two · Dec 2024

Expanding the Care

The promise was kept. In 2024, We Remember You grew to support 50 women with comprehensive programming — medical screenings, a catered day of care, and partnerships with shelters in Enugu and Lagos.

Year Three

Beyond One Program

By year three, the pattern was unmistakable. We Remember You kept growing — but so did something else: story after story of Nigerians abroad already doing this same kind of work in their own way, one-off and grassroots, generous and disconnected from anything built to last.

The Bigger Realization

One program isn't an ecosystem.

Across the diaspora, so many of us are already doing this work — a cousin quietly covering school fees, a friend group shipping medical supplies, a hometown association raising money for a borehole. All of it real. All of it generous. And almost none of it connected to anything built to last.

That's the gap Hearts Agree Foundation exists to close: not just giving, but building the ecosystem underneath the giving — the skills, the resources, the thinking, and the resolve it takes for sustainable programs and real advocacy to take root and last.

There is a difference between charity and investment. Charity asks nothing of the future. Investment builds it.

What This Means for Us

We Remember You stays the flagship. The mission has grown around it.

We Remember You remains our flagship community care program — it's the proof of what showing up for each other looks like, and it isn't going anywhere. But our mission is no longer just about repeating that one program at a larger scale each year. It's about building the ecosystem that lets every version of that work — ours and everyone else's — become sustainable.

That's why the Hearts Agree Task Force and the Hearts Agree Entrepreneur Fund aren't side projects — they're core to what we're building. The Task Force convenes the diaspora leaders already doing this work, and the Entrepreneur Fund puts real resources behind them, so one-off generosity can grow into coordinated, lasting development.
